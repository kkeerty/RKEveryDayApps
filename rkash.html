Quick answers

Common questions

How do I add income or an expense?

Use the add control in RKash, choose the appropriate transaction type, enter the amount and details, then save.

Can I edit or remove a transaction?

Open the transaction to see available editing options. Confirm carefully before deleting financial records.

Does RKash connect to my bank?

This answer should be updated to match the app’s actual account-linking behavior before publishing.

Is RKash financial advice?

No. RKash is a personal organization tool and does not provide financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

What should I include in a support request?

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Never email account passwords or sensitive financial information.
