Everyday use

Organize what matters

Open any topic for clear, step-by-step help.

✓TasksAdd, find, complete, repeat, or remove a task.Open guide
  1. Open Tasks and tap Add to create a task.
  2. Tap an existing task whenever you need to change its details.
  3. Choose a category such as Family, Medical, or Bills to keep tasks organized.
  4. Use Search, choose a category, or select Current, All, or Closed to find what you need.
  5. Tap the status control to close or reopen a task. Swipe left to delete it, or use Delete Task while editing.
  6. For something that happens regularly, choose a repeat period. Completing the current occurrence creates the next one while recurrence is active.
🛒ShoppingKeep shopping items separate from normal tasks.Open guide
  1. Open Shopping and tap Add to create a list item.
  2. Tap an item to edit it, or swipe left to delete it.
  3. Mark an item Purchased when you buy it.
  4. If an item is recurring, it can appear again at its next scheduled time.
♡Special DatesRemember birthdays, anniversaries, and other dates.Open guide
  1. Use Special Dates for dates you want to remember. Unlike tasks, they do not need to be marked complete.
  2. When adding or editing, choose Birthday, Anniversary, or Other.
  3. Special Dates repeat every year by default. You can choose another available repeat period.
  4. Turn on Pause Recurring if you want to keep the date but stop creating future occurrences.
◷RemindersSet reminders and check notification access.Open guide
  1. Choose a reminder while adding or editing an item.
  2. Reminders depend on notification permission and your iPhone settings.
  3. If reminders do not appear, open the iPhone Settings app, find RKji, and confirm that Notifications are allowed.
  4. Also check Focus modes and notification delivery settings.

Voice and reading

Speak, ask, or listen

●Create by voiceSay what you want RKji to add.Open guide

Tap the microphone and speak naturally. Try:

  • “Add task call John tomorrow at 10 AM.”
  • “Add milk to list weekly recurring.”
  • “Add birthday of KK January 1st.”
  • “Add anniversary June 20th.”

Words such as Task, List, Birthday, or Anniversary tell RKji what kind of item to create. Otherwise, RKji uses the screen you are currently viewing. A detail screen opens so you can review everything before tapping Save.

?Ask RKjiUse a voice question to find information.Open guide

Tap the microphone and try:

  • “What are today’s tasks?”
  • “What are my medical tasks?”
  • “What are my billing tasks?”
  • “What’s on September 30th?”

Task questions filter the Tasks screen. A question about a specific date can show Tasks and Special Dates. When you say “Calendar,” RKji means information saved in RKji, not Apple Calendar.

🔊Read aloudListen to the information currently shown.Open guide
  1. Tap the speaker button where it is shown to read the current information aloud.
  2. In Settings, turn on Read voice-query results aloud if you want voice answers spoken automatically.
  3. When that setting is off, voice-query results appear silently on the screen. The manual Read button remains available where shown.

Family Spaces

Share selected items

＋Create or join a spaceInvite family or accept an invitation.Open guide
  1. Open Settings, then Family Spaces.
  2. Create a space and choose Invite or Manage People to invite someone through iCloud sharing.
  3. To join someone else’s space, accept the Family Space invitation they send you.
↔Personal or sharedChoose where each item is saved.Open guide
  1. When adding or editing, choose Personal or a Family Space.
  2. Personal items remain personal. Items saved in a Family Space are shared with its participants.
  3. Shared changes may take a little time to appear while iCloud synchronizes.
👥Owner and MemberUnderstand who manages the space.Open guide

The person who creates a Family Space is its Owner. The Owner can rename the space, manage invitations, or permanently delete the space and its items for everyone. A Member joins through an invitation and can manage or leave a joined space.

Comfort and readability

Make RKji easier to read

Open Settings in RKji to choose gentle colors, larger text, defaults for new items, and whether voice-query results are read aloud automatically.

You can also use the speaker button wherever it appears to read the information currently shown.

Quick answers

Common questions

Why did completing a task create another one?

The task has an active repeat period. Completing the current occurrence creates the next one. Edit the recurring task if you want to change or pause its recurrence.

Why does a Special Date stay open?

Special Dates are dates to remember rather than things to complete. They do not need to be marked complete.

Why did RKji create the wrong kind of item from my voice?

Include Task, List, Birthday, or Anniversary in your command. Those words override the current screen. Always review the detail screen before tapping Save.

Why are my voice-query results not spoken?

Open RKji Settings and turn on Read voice-query results aloud. If it remains off, results are displayed silently and you can use the manual Read button where shown.

Why is a reminder not appearing?

Confirm that RKji can send notifications in the iPhone Settings app. Also review Focus modes and notification delivery settings. Reminders depend on device settings and technical availability.

Why has a shared item not appeared yet?

Family Space items use iCloud sharing and may take some time to synchronize. Confirm that both devices have an internet connection and are signed in to iCloud.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

Read the RKji Privacy Policy for information about storage, sharing, permissions, and your choices.

Important Information

Keep a backup plan for critical information

RKji is a personal organization and reminder tool. It should not be relied upon for medical, emergency, financial, legal, or other critical notifications.

Reminders, synchronization, sharing, and stored information may be affected by device settings, network availability, iCloud availability, or other technical conditions. Keep independent records of information that is important to you.
