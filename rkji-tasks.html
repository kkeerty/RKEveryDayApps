RKji Tasks helps you remember everyday tasks, birthdays, and shopping items, whether they are just for you or shared with family and friends. Step-by-step guides

Learn at your pace

Open a guide to read the steps, or have your device read the instructions aloud.

+Type or speakCreate a task manually or with your voice.Open guide
  1. Tap Add to type a task, or tap the microphone to speak.
  2. For voice entry, include the task and timing in one sentence—for example, “Take medication every day at 9 AM.”
  3. Stop listening when you finish speaking.
  4. Review the task name, type, due date, reminder, notes, contact, and repeat schedule.
  5. Tap Save when everything looks right.

To create a task in RKji, tap Add to type, or tap the microphone to speak. When speaking, include the task and timing in one sentence. Stop listening when you finish. Review the task name, type, due date, reminder, notes, contact, and repeat schedule. When everything looks right, tap Save.

◷Plan and rememberSchedule tasks and helpful reminders.Open guide
  1. Open a task or create a new one.
  2. Choose a type such as Personal, Family, Medical, Household, or Bills.
  3. Add a due date and time.
  4. Choose a reminder: 5, 15, or 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 1 day before it is due.
  5. If needed, make the task repeat every day, week, or month.
  6. Use the Current, All, or Closed filter to find the tasks you need.

To plan a task, open it or create a new one. Choose a task type, then add a due date and time. Choose a reminder from five minutes to one day before the task is due. If needed, set the task to repeat every day, week, or month. Use the Current, All, or Closed filter to find the tasks you need.

☁Share with familyCreate a private iCloud Family Space.Open guide
  1. Open Settings, then choose Family Spaces.
  2. Enter the name you want shown in shared-task activity.
  3. Tap Create Family Space and give the space a simple name.
  4. Choose Invite or Manage People and send the iCloud invitation.
  5. When creating a task, choose the Family Space under Save In.
  6. Keep private items in Personal; they are not shared with the Family Space.

To share tasks, open Settings and choose Family Spaces. Enter the name you want shown in shared activity. Create a Family Space and give it a simple name. Choose Invite or Manage People and send the iCloud invitation. When creating a task, choose the Family Space under Save In. Keep private items in Personal, where they are not shared.

🎂Remember birthdaysKeep birthdays together and receive a reminder.Open guide
  1. Tap the birthday button, then tap Add.
  2. Enter the person’s name and birthday.
  3. Add the birth year if you would like RKji to show the age they will turn.
  4. Add a contact if you want a convenient call button.
  5. Choose Personal or a Family Space, then tap Save.
  6. RKji sets the birthday to return every year and, unless you change it, reminds you one day beforehand.

To remember a birthday, tap the birthday button and then tap Add. Enter the person’s name and birthday. You may also add the birth year so RKji can show the age they will turn, and add a contact for a convenient call button. Choose Personal or a Family Space, then tap Save. RKji brings the birthday back every year and normally reminds you one day beforehand.

🛒Use the shopping listPlan purchases and quickly reuse previous items.Open guide
  1. Tap the shopping-cart button to open Shopping.
  2. Tap Add, enter the item, and optionally add the store.
  3. Choose whether the item should return every day, week, month, or year.
  4. Use To Buy while shopping, then mark an item purchased when you have it.
  5. Open Purchased to add one or several previous items again without retyping them.
  6. Use Upcoming to see recurring items that are not needed yet, move one to To Buy, or skip it this time.

To use the shopping list, tap the shopping-cart button and then tap Add. Enter the item and, if helpful, the store. Choose whether it should return on a schedule. Use To Buy while shopping and mark the item purchased when you have it. Purchased lets you add previous items again without retyping them. Upcoming shows recurring items that are not needed yet. You can move one to To Buy or skip it this time.

Quick answers

Common questions

How are tasks organized?

The task list groups items into Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, and Later. Use the Current, All, and Closed filters at the bottom of the screen, or search by task name.

How do I add a task by voice?

Tap the microphone, speak the task and timing, then stop listening. RKji can recognize a date and daily, weekly, or monthly repetition. Always review the task name, type, date, reminder, notes, and contact before saving.

What kinds of tasks can I create?

Choose Kids, Medical, Family, Household, Personal, Bills, Misc, Birthday, or List. Birthdays and List items also have their own simple screens and tools.

How do reminders work?

Choose no reminder or receive one 5, 15, or 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 1 day before the due time. Tap a notification to open the task. Press and hold it to close the task or be reminded again in 30 minutes.

How do repeating tasks work?

A task can repeat every day, week, month, or year. When you close the current occurrence, RKji keeps it in your history and creates the next open occurrence. Closing a repeating task from its edit screen pauses the recurring task.

How do birthdays work?

Add the person’s name and birthday. A birth year is optional; when included, RKji shows the age the person will turn. Birthdays return automatically each year and use a one-day reminder by default. Open the Birthdays screen to browse them by year or hear them read aloud.

How does the shopping list work?

Shopping is divided into To Buy, Purchased, and Upcoming. Add an optional store and make an item recurring if you buy it regularly. Purchased items stay available so you can add one or several again without retyping. Upcoming items can be moved to To Buy or skipped for the current cycle.

Can RKji read my tasks aloud?

Yes. Tap the speaker button to hear the tasks currently shown on screen. Search and filters determine which tasks are read.

Can I attach a person to a task?

Yes. Add a contact name and phone number manually or choose someone from Contacts. A call button appears with the task when a valid phone number is available.

How does family sharing work?

In Settings, open Family Spaces. You can create or join more than one space and invite people through iCloud. Shared-task activity can show who created, updated, or closed a task. Personal tasks remain private.

Where is my task data stored?

RKji stores personal and shared task data using Apple Core Data and iCloud/CloudKit. Family Space participants receive only the data shared with that space through Apple’s iCloud sharing system.

Can I change the task-list appearance?

Yes. Settings includes Gentle Section Colors and Larger Task Text. For larger text throughout the app, adjust Text Size in iPhone Settings under Display & Brightness.

How do I delete a task?

Swipe a task row to the left and tap Delete, or open a task and use Delete Task. Only the Family Space owner can move or delete certain shared items.

What should I include in a support request?

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, RKji version, whether the task is Personal or in a Family Space, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot is helpful when available.
